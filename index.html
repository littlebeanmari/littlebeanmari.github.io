Standings details

Phase 2 and Top Cut: placements up to the Top Cut number are marked gold and the following placements up to the Phase 2 number are marked blue. In the JPG and HTML this is a colored bar with a legend; in the plain text and Discord outputs the players are grouped under headings. Dropped players are not marked.

Players

CSV columns: place, player, record (for example 5-1-0), points and, optionally, the round the player dropped and up to six Pokémon names (TCG uses the first two, VGC and GO up to six). A header row is optional.

Players with a round in the "Dropped" column are listed at the bottom, ordered by that round, grayed out and in italics. The round itself is only used for sorting and is never shown in the outputs.
