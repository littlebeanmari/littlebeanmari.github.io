Schedule details

Only tournaments inside this time frame appear in the output. Leave empty to include everything.

Tournaments

CSV columns: date, time, game, type, fee, store, and optionally time zone (e.g. Europe/Berlin), tournament name, link URL and password, followed by any extra columns in the order they appear in the table. Date as YYYY-MM-DD. A header row is optional.

Untick the checkbox at the start of a tournament to leave it out of the outputs. Click the Store or Password header to rename it. "Add column" creates an extra column after Link; its name can be edited as well. Column names are saved in this browser.

Click an entry to unselect every tournament that has it, and click it again to select them again. Entries with only some tournaments selected show a dashed outline.

Social Media

Enter a username or a full link. Platforms you fill in appear as logos at the bottom of the JPG graphic and as clickable icons below the HTML table.

Look

The background colours only apply to the JPG. The HTML output keeps a transparent background. Rows are drawn slightly see-through, so pick a dark row colour with light text, or a light row colour with dark text.

Row colours

Choose a tab, then pick a colour for each entry in it. Tournaments with that entry get the colour as their background in the JPG and HTML outputs. Double Header is listed under Type once a store runs a Challenge and a Cup of the same game on the same day. If a tournament has more than one coloured entry, Double Header comes first, then Type, Game, Store and the extra columns.

Standings details

Phase 2 and Top Cut: placements up to the Top Cut number are marked gold and the following placements up to the Phase 2 number are marked blue. In the JPG and HTML this is a colored bar with a legend; in the plain text and Discord outputs the players are grouped under headings. Dropped players are not marked.

Bracket

The bracket is seeded from the Players table: the best placements come first and dropped players are skipped. If the bracket has fewer players than its size, the top seeds get a bye. Data loaded or entered in the Standings tab is used automatically.

Match results (optional)

Pick the winner of a match to move that player into the next round. The score is optional.

Player

Add one line per round in the Matchups table below: the round, your opponent, the result (for example Win, Loss or Tie, or just W, L or T) and the table. The opponent's Pokémon are optional. The result column is colored green, red or amber, and your record is calculated for the subtitle.

The import uses the player name above. For the best result, import twice: first paste the standings data (the link from the Standings tab works the same way) and click the button, then open the pairings in step 2, paste them and click the button again. The standings tell the tool your opponents' names and Pokémon.

Usage

Percentages are calculated from the Players table below: the share of players in each group who played the card or Pokémon. Groups are formed from the placements, using the Phase 2 and Top Cut numbers above. Dropped players are not counted in Phase 2 or Top Cut. For TCG, usage is counted per deck archetype, taken from each player’s Pokémon 1 and 2 (the Limitless import fills these in automatically).

Manual entries and overrides

Add an entry or a percentage to replace what was calculated. Percentages that you type win over the calculated ones. In the last column you can replace the picture that was found: type other Pokémon names (for a TCG deck, up to two, separated by commas) or paste a direct link to an image.

NameImage override (name or URL)

Players

CSV columns: place, player, record (for example 5-1-0), points and, optionally, the round the player dropped and up to six Pokémon names (TCG uses the first two, VGC and GO up to six). A header row is optional.

Players with a round in the "Dropped" column are listed at the bottom, ordered by that round, grayed out and in italics. The round itself is only used for sorting and is never shown in the outputs.

Your data stays on your device. To save you retyping, this tool remembers some of your inputs (the tournaments and row colours on the Schedule tab, the Limitless links and pasted data, your custom colours, and your social media links) in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, nothing is shared, and no cookies are used. The only outside requests are for pictures, such as Pokémon sprites, which are looked up by Pokémon name. To delete the saved data, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
